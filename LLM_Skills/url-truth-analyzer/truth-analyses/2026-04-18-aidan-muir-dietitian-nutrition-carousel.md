**Claim 3: Metabolic adaptation effects (Image 5)**
"Hunger increases over time, Cravings increase, Metabolic adaptation reduces BMR, Calories burned through non-exercise activity often decreases"
**STRONGLY SUPPORTED.** All four points describe well-documented components of metabolic adaptation to caloric restriction:
- Hunger/cravings increase: leptin and ghrelin dysregulation during energy deficit (Sumithran et al., NEJM 2011)
- Metabolic adaptation reduces BMR: adaptive thermogenesis — BMR can decrease 100-400 kcal/day beyond what weight loss alone predicts (Rosenbaum & Leibel, 2010)
- NEAT decreases: non-exercise activity thermogenesis decreases substantially during caloric restriction, contributing significantly to metabolic adaptation (Levine, Science 1999)

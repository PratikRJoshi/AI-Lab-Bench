## Analysis

### SORT Analysis

**Claim**: If you have Hashimoto's with puffiness, brain fog, and food reactivity, and gut work/diet/supplements aren't helping, there's a hidden pattern that explains it.

#### Safety Lens
- "Puffiness for no reason" in a Hashimoto's patient should trigger evaluation of hypothyroid status (myxedema), medication dose adequacy, and renal function — not functional medicine protocols.
- Brain fog in Hashimoto's is most commonly caused by undertreated hypothyroidism (suboptimal FT3 levels). This is treatable with medication adjustment.
- "Reacting to everything" (presumably food sensitivities) could indicate mast cell activation, IgE-mediated allergy, or anxiety-driven symptom attribution. Each requires different evaluation.
- The post offers no specific diagnostic guidance, just "I see this pattern" → implicit: come to me.

#### Outcomes Lens
- No outcomes data. No claim about what the solution is or what results it produces.
- The entire post is a problem-statement without solution — designed to drive engagement (comments/DMs) not educate.

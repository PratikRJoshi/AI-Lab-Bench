## Analysis

### SORT Analysis

#### Claim 1: "Tired/Wired/Bloated/Weight gain = one problem (Hashimoto's root cause)"
- **Safety**: Attributing four non-specific symptoms to a single cause discourages differential diagnosis. Fatigue + insomnia + bloating + weight gain could indicate: depression, sleep apnea, PCOS, Cushing's syndrome, medication side effects, or simply lifestyle factors.
- **Outcomes**: No evidence cited. This is marketing framing (reduce complexity → offer single solution).
- **Bias**: Classic funnel marketing: create identification with symptoms → propose unified explanation → sell program.
- **SORT Grade**: C — Expert opinion, no evidence cited.

#### Claim 2: "Binder supplements alone don't clear mycotoxins from Hashimoto's bodies"
- **Safety**: Mycotoxin testing (urine mycotoxin panels) has high false-positive rates. The labs most functional practitioners use (RealTime Labs, Great Plains/Mosaic) have been criticized for lacking specificity and clinical validation.
- **Outcomes**: DOE only — mycotoxin levels are surrogate markers. No RCT demonstrates that "clearing mycotoxins" improves Hashimoto's patient-oriented outcomes.
- **Bias**: The mycotoxin-autoimmune connection is largely promoted by functional medicine practitioners who also sell the testing and treatment protocols. Circular business model.
- **Total evidence**: While mycotoxins (especially ochratoxin A and aflatoxins) have immunomodulatory properties in animal studies, the claim that they drive Hashimoto's specifically lacks human clinical trial evidence.
- **SORT Grade**: C — Mechanistic reasoning from animal data extrapolated to humans, no clinical trials.

#### Claim 3: "Calories in calories out was never designed for a Hashimoto's body"
- **Safety**: Discouraging caloric awareness in a population prone to weight gain (hypothyroid patients) could worsen metabolic outcomes.
- **Outcomes**: Thermodynamics applies to all human bodies. Hashimoto's patients may have reduced BMR (100-300 kcal/day lower when undertreated), but CICO still governs weight change — the "CI" and "CO" values just differ.
- **Bias**: This is a straw-man argument. CICO is a physical law, not a "design" for specific bodies. The real issue is that Hashimoto's patients need thyroid hormone optimization to normalize their metabolic rate, after which standard energy balance applies.
- **Total evidence**: Multiple studies confirm that adequately treated hypothyroid patients have normal metabolic rates and respond to caloric interventions normally (Samuels et al., Thyroid, 2018).
- **SORT Grade**: C — Contradicts established physiology. No evidence that energy balance is suspended in Hashimoto's.

#### Claim 4: Before/after transformation implies her program reverses Hashimoto's
- **Safety**: "Remission" in Hashimoto's is not a standard medical term in this context. Hashimoto's is a chronic autoimmune condition. Antibody levels fluctuate naturally. Weight changes could reflect many interventions.
- **Bias**: Transformation photos are the strongest form of marketing bias — survivorship bias (only successful clients shown), no controlled comparison, timeline and confounders unknown.
- **SORT Grade**: C — Anecdotal testimonial evidence.

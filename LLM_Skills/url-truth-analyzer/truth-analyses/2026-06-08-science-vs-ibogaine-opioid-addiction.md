## Analysis

### Science / EBM
The clip's nuanced position is exactly right. Reviews confirm that all human efficacy data for ibogaine in substance-use disorder come from **open-label observational studies, retrospective analyses, and case reports — no randomized placebo-controlled trials** [1][2]. These do suggest reduced withdrawal and craving (hence the "promise"), but without controls and with non-lasting effects, "cures 90%" is unsupported as a definitive efficacy claim. On safety, the literature is unambiguous: ibogaine and its metabolite noribogaine block hERG potassium channels, causing concentration-dependent QT-interval prolongation that can trigger malignant ventricular arrhythmias (torsades de pointes), with documented fatalities — a cardiac risk profile that distinguishes it from typical serotonergic psychedelics [1][2]. So both halves of the clip — real promise, real (cardiac) danger, no silver bullet — are accurate.

## ELI5 — Friend to Friend
Ibogaine isn't snake oil — people coming off opioids really do report it helps. But "cures 90%!" is hype: there's never been a proper placebo trial, the effect can wear off, and — the scary part Rogan skips — it can mess with your heart rhythm badly enough to kill you. So: promising, not magic, and genuinely dangerous without medical monitoring. Science Vs got the balance right.

### SORT Analysis

| Claim | Verdict | Grade |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Hashimoto's can complicate weight loss beyond simple CICO | **Supported** — hypothyroidism reduces metabolic rate; well-established | A-B |

**Overall SORT Grade: C** — Mixes legitimate metabolic concepts with unsupported functional medicine claims, all designed to convince you that standard doctors are missing "the real problem" and you need her specialized (paid) testing.

## Verdict
The post takes real metabolic science (T3 drives metabolism, insulin resistance is under-recognized) and mixes it with unsupported claims (mycotoxin-driven weight gain) to create the impression that only functional practitioners can solve your weight loss. Standard endocrinology already tests most of these markers. The post manufactures a problem that standard medicine supposedly can't solve.

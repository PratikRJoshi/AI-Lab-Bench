## Analysis

### Claim Validation

1. **Average British family throws away £700 of food per year** — SUPPORTED. WRAP's published data consistently places UK household food waste costs in the £600–£800 range annually.

2. **Milk can be frozen in ice cube trays and lasts 3 months** — SUPPORTED. The NHS and Love Food Hate Waste confirm milk freezes safely for up to 3–6 months.

3. **Frozen butter can be grated directly for baking, producing flakier pastry** — SUPPORTED. This is a well-known baking technique; cold fat distribution is key to flaky pastry, confirmed by baking science sources.

4. **Herbs frozen in olive oil cubes preserve color and flavor** — SUPPORTED. The oil layer prevents freezer burn and oxidation. Widely recommended by food preservation experts.

5. **WRAP found the average UK household has £50 of forgotten food in the freezer** — CONTESTED. WRAP has published freezer waste figures, but the exact £50 figure is difficult to verify from their primary reports. The general point about freezer waste is valid.

6. **UK households waste 6.4 million tonnes of food annually** — SUPPORTED. WRAP's 2023 data reports approximately 6.4 million tonnes of household food waste in the UK.

7. **Supermarkets package chicken in packs of 4 because families of 2 will still buy them and throw 2 away** — CONTESTED. While supermarkets do use packaging strategies to increase basket size, the specific claim about deliberate waste-inducing packaging is an oversimplification of complex retail decisions.

8. **Yellow sticker meat reduced 50–75% is safe to freeze immediately** — SUPPORTED. The Food Standards Agency confirms that food at its sell-by date is safe to freeze if done promptly, extending its usable life.

9. **Cooked rice can be safely frozen and reheated** — SUPPORTED. The NHS confirms cooked rice is safe if cooled within an hour and reheated thoroughly to steaming hot.

10. **Batch cooking can cut weekly food spend from £115 to £60–70** — CONTESTED. While batch cooking and freezing clearly reduces costs, a 40–50% reduction depends heavily on family size, starting habits, and meal choices. The £115 average weekly spend figure aligns with ONS data, but the savings projection is an optimistic best case.

## Verdict
The vast majority of the 25 tips are practical, safe, and well-grounded in food science and preservation principles. The core message — that strategic freezing reduces food waste and saves money — is strongly supported by WRAP data and government food safety guidance. Where the video stretches credibility is in some of the specific savings figures (which are optimistic estimates rather than verified data) and the conspiratorial framing around supermarket packaging practices. Overall, it's a useful and mostly accurate guide to freezer management.

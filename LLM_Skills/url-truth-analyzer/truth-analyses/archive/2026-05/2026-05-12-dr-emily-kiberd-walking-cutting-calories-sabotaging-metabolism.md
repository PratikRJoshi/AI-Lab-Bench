## Analysis

### SORT Analysis

| Claim | Verdict | Grade |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Excessive calorie restriction can slow metabolism | **Supported** — adaptive thermogenesis is well-documented; extreme restriction downregulates T3 and metabolic rate | A-B |
| Walking alone is insufficient for body recomposition | **Partially supported** — resistance training provides superior metabolic benefits over walking alone; but walking IS beneficial and shouldn't be dismissed | B |
| Hashimoto's women are "unknowingly sabotaging" with moderate approaches | **Overstated** — moderate calorie reduction + walking is the baseline recommendation from ATA and most endocrinologists; it works for many patients | C |

**Overall SORT Grade: C** — Uses a real phenomenon (metabolic adaptation) to fearmonger against standard medical advice, creating the impression that only her specialized approach works.

## Verdict
Metabolic adaptation to calorie restriction is real, and adding resistance training to walking is genuinely good advice. But positioning standard medical recommendations (moderate calorie reduction + walking) as "sabotage" is a marketing scare tactic, not medical guidance.

## ELI5 — Friend to Friend
She's saying that walking and eating less is secretly hurting you. That's not really true — those are perfectly fine starting points. She's right that adding weight lifting helps, but she's wrong to call walking "sabotage." She's trying to make standard medical advice sound dangerous so you buy her approach instead.

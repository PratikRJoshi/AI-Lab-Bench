## Analysis

### SORT Analysis — Each Habit

**1. Flossing (or equivalent interdental cleaning)**
- Outcomes: Patient-oriented (periodontal disease, tooth loss, possibly CVD via oral-systemic pathway).
- Evidence: Cochrane review 2019 (Worthington et al.) found low-to-moderate-certainty evidence that flossing + brushing reduces gingivitis vs. brushing alone; benefit for plaque modest. ADA 2016 and USPSTF continue to recommend daily interdental cleaning.
- **SORT: B** (inconsistent POEMs — real but smaller effect than commonly implied; interdental brushes may outperform floss per Poklepovic 2013 Cochrane).

**2. Stretching**
- Outcomes: Mixed. Helps maintain range of motion and reduces injury risk in some populations (older adults, overhead athletes); does NOT reliably reduce muscle soreness (Herbert & de Noronha, Cochrane 2011).
- **SORT: B** for ROM/flexibility as POEM; **C** for injury prevention (inconsistent).

**3. Sunscreen**
- Outcomes: Patient-oriented — reduces squamous cell carcinoma, actinic keratoses, melanoma, and photoaging.
- Evidence: Nambour trial (Green et al., JCO 2011, 4.5-year RCT + 8-year follow-up) — daily sunscreen use reduced invasive melanoma incidence by 50% and SCC by 40%. USPSTF "B" recommendation for behavioral counseling in fair-skinned individuals.
- **SORT: A** — consistent POEMs from RCT.

**4. Drinking water**
- Outcomes: Proper hydration matters for thermoregulation, cognition at extremes, kidney stone prevention. The "8 glasses/day" heuristic is NOT evidence-based — fluid needs vary by body size, climate, activity, diet (much intake comes from food). Mild dehydration ≠ major health outcomes in healthy adults (Perrier et al., Nutrients 2020).
- **SORT: B** for kidney stone prevention in stone-formers (RCT evidence, Borghi 1996); **C** for generic "drink more water = better health" in healthy adults.

**5. Going to bed earlier / adequate sleep**
- Outcomes: Large patient-oriented literature. 7–9 hours for adults is associated with lower all-cause mortality, cardiovascular events, metabolic disease, and better cognition (Cappuccio 2010 meta-analysis; AHA Life's Essential 8 adds sleep in 2022).
- **SORT: A** — consistent POEMs across prospective cohorts and some interventional trials.

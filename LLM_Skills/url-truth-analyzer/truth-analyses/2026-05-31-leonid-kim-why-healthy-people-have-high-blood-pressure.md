## Analysis

### Medical: SORT Analysis
This is clinically sophisticated and notably current. The primary-aldosteronism section (C1) reflects a genuine paradigm shift: PA is now recognized as common in resistant hypertension, frequently presents with *normal* potassium (overturning the old screening trigger), and recent Endocrine Society guidance moves toward broader screening via the aldosterone-renin ratio — all accurate and ahead of average clinical practice. The homocysteine/B-vitamin section (C2) is correctly hedged: folate lowers BP and CV events *specifically when homocysteine is elevated* (a folic-acid meta-analysis supports ~8/7 mmHg reductions and ~13% fewer CV events as adjunct), not universally — and he notes the MTHFR variant. The insulin/visceral-fat/inflammation triad (C3) and the insulin→renal-sodium-retention mechanism are accurate, as is the recommendation to check fasting insulin early. The sleep-apnea section (C4: OSA in ~30–50% of HTN) is well-supported. Appropriately defers to physician testing/treatment throughout. Reasonable Grade B overall (guideline + RCT-adjacent + mechanistic).

## Analysis

### Science: Claim Validation
The physics is mainstream and largely accurate. Quantum-field-theory framing (particles as field excitations; identical/indistinguishable electrons; virtual particles; the 2012 Higgs discovery confirming a pervasive scalar field) is standard. The cosmology is careful and correct: gravitational + velocity time dilation, no universal "now," the CMB and cosmic structure as approximate clocks, and especially the three horizons — his quoted figures (cosmic event horizon ~16 Gly, Hubble sphere ~14 Gly, particle horizon ~46 Gly) match the canonical Davis & Lineweaver treatment almost exactly (C2). Quantum entanglement "measured from Earth to a satellite" is correct — the Micius experiment distributed entanglement over 1,203 km (C1).

Two items deserve nuance: (a) "everything moves through spacetime at the speed of light" (C4) is a legitimate, widely used *pedagogical* framing of the four-velocity, not a literal measurable speed — fine as a teaching device, not a standalone fact; (b) framing Planck "super-horizon fluctuations" as having "reached experimental verification of the multiverse" (C5) is an overstatement — such correlations are consistent with inflation but are not accepted as verification of a multiverse. Crucially, Oluseyi himself says he is "not convinced it's conclusive," which keeps the segment honest. A couple of caption glitches (e.g., "2.5 billion" where "2.5 million light-years" to Andromeda is meant) are transcription artifacts; he states the correct value elsewhere.

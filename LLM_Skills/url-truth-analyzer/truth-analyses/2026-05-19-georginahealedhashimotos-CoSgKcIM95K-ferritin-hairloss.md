Hidden risk is what the unseen answer might be: iron supplementation without first confirming deficiency on labs (serum ferritin, transferrin saturation, CBC) is not benign — iron overload (hemochromatosis carriers, anemia of chronic disease, infection), GI side effects, and missed underlying causes (occult GI blood loss, celiac, atrophic gastritis, heavy menses) are real harms.

**Channel-level commentary on the "lower inflammation" framing (for context, not as a per-claim verdict):**
"Inflammation" in social-media wellness content typically conflates (a) acute inflammation (productive, time-limited host response — not a target of lifestyle "lowering"), (b) chronic low-grade systemic inflammation as measured by hs-CRP/IL-6 (modifiable by diet, sleep, exercise, weight, smoking, and treatment of specific conditions), and (c) tissue-specific autoimmune inflammation (e.g., thyroiditis, IBD, RA — driven by antigen-specific adaptive immunity, only partially responsive to lifestyle). Lifestyle measures with the best evidence for lowering hs-CRP/IL-6 — a Mediterranean-style dietary pattern, regular aerobic activity, adequate sleep, smoking cessation, weight loss in those with obesity, and treatment of insulin resistance — are real but not "quick" and not interchangeable with disease-modifying therapy. The "5 quick & easy" framing is itself the clinical red flag, independent of whatever the specific 5 items happen to be.

## Analysis

### Science / EBM
The episode's claims match the evidence precisely. BPC-157 and similar "wellness" peptides have extensive **animal** data but only a handful of small human studies — none meeting drug-approval standards — so human efficacy is essentially unproven [1][2]. The FDA moved 19 such peptides to Category 2 in 2023 citing immunogenicity, manufacturing impurities, and lack of human trial data; BPC-157 is also FDA-unapproved and WADA-prohibited [1][2]. The contamination concern is real and documented: independent testing of gray-market "research chemical" peptides finds incorrect sequences, endotoxins, heavy metals (lead), and inaccurate dosing, and injecting them risks infection/abscess [1][2]. The "10–20% of samples have issues" figure is one lab's estimate (Dr. Drabkin's), so it's anecdotal in magnitude, but it's directionally consistent with the broader literature on unregulated peptide quality.

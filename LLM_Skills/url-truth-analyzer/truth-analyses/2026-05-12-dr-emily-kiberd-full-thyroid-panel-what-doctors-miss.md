## Verdict
Ironically the most useful post from this account. Yes, an expanded thyroid panel provides better data than TSH alone. But framing this as something doctors are hiding from you — rather than something any endocrinologist already does — creates unnecessary distrust and drives patients toward functional practitioners instead of qualified specialists.

## ELI5 — Friend to Friend
She's actually right on this one — if you have Hashimoto's, getting more than just TSH tested is a good idea. But don't go to Instagram for this — just ask your doctor for a referral to an endocrinologist. They'll order the full panel. It's not a secret they're keeping from you.

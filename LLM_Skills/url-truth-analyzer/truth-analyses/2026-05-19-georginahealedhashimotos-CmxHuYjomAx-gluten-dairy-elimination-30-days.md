### Medical: SORT Analysis

#### Pass 1 — Four lenses on the implicit claim ("30 days of gluten + dairy elimination is a meaningful test of dietary intervention for Hashimoto's")

- **Safety**: Short-term elimination of gluten and dairy is generally safe in adults with no concurrent eating disorder, growth concerns, or osteoporosis risk. No acute safety harm from a 30-day n=1 trial. Indirect harm comes from **drawing premature negative conclusions** from a 30-day window and then escalating into more aggressive interventions (mycotoxin "detox," extended elimination diets, deprescribing levothyroxine).
- **Outcomes (POEM vs DOE)**: The claim is a self-reported *symptom* outcome (POEM-flavored) over 30 days. The peer-reviewed literature on diet and Hashimoto's measures **anti-TPO/anti-Tg antibody titers and TSH/fT4** (DOEs), typically over **8–24 weeks**, not 4 weeks. So even a properly-run 30-day n=1 wouldn't be expected to register on the metrics the science actually tracks.
- **Risk of bias**: Single-subject, unblinded, no measured baseline antibody titer, no controlled re-introduction, retrospective social-media reporting, monetization conflict of interest. Maximum bias.
- **Total evidence**: Anti-inflammatory and gluten-free diets show **modest reductions in anti-TPO** in some small RCTs and observational studies, particularly in patients with concurrent celiac/NCGS. Effect sizes are inconsistent and not always statistically significant. No major endocrine guideline (ATA, Endocrine Society, NICE) recommends routine gluten or dairy elimination for Hashimoto's without comorbid celiac disease.

#### Pass 2 — SORT grade
- **Grade C** for the implicit clinical claim that gluten + dairy elimination is a meaningful intervention worth testing for general Hashimoto's patients. Evidence is mixed, small-study, surrogate-endpoint, and not in guidelines. The 30-day window in particular is too short to be informative.
- The *narrow* anecdotal claim "I personally did not feel better in 30 days" is unfalsifiable / unscientific by construction — it can be true while saying nothing about the population.

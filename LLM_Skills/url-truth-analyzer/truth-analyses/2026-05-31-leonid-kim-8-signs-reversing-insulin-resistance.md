## Analysis

### Medical: SORT Analysis
Each of the 8 signs is tied to a correct, mainstream mechanism: insulin's antinatriuretic effect on the kidney (sodium/water retention → early "whoosh" of water weight); central glucose access and craving normalization; glycemic-swing-driven postprandial fatigue and mitochondrial efficiency; brain insulin receptors + inflammation for cognition; the bidirectional sleep↔insulin loop; insulin's role in visceral fat partitioning; endothelial dysfunction and blood pressure; and the dermatologic markers (acanthosis nigricans, skin tags, androgen-driven acne) that are textbook insulin-resistance signs. The two named studies are real and apt: short sleep restriction (~4 h × 5 nights) reducing insulin sensitivity ~25% by clamp (C5), and ~5% weight loss yielding 15–25% visceral-fat reduction, amplified by exercise (C6). The framing — "early signs precede lab changes; don't live by the scale" — is clinically sound and motivational without overpromising. Reasonable Grade B overall (mechanistic + observational + small RCTs).

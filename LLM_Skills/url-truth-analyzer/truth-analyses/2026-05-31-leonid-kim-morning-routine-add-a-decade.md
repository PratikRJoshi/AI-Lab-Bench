## Analysis

### Medical: SORT Analysis
Each habit maps to a real, large study, presented as association: the **Million Veteran Program** (8 factors → up to ~24 yr life-expectancy gap at 40); nuts (NEJM, ~76k adults, 20% lower all-cause mortality at ≥7×/wk); coffee (JAMA Internal Medicine, ~12% lower mortality, decaf comparable → not just caffeine); green tea (large Japanese pooled cohort, ~11% lower cancer death); social isolation/loneliness (33%/57% higher mortality); morning light → SCN → melatonin timing; short sleep (<7 h, ~14% higher mortality); walking (Taiwan ~400k, 15 min/day → 14% lower mortality + ~3 yr). All are mainstream and correctly framed as observational signals with a dose-response ("each habit adds benefit; not all-or-nothing"). The breakfast section (protein+fiber flatten glucose, GLP-1 analogy to GLP-1 drugs, fiber-to-carb ratio rule) is accurate and includes a sensible hydration caveat for fiber. No overreach; the "20 extra years" is correctly attributed to the MVP study's extreme contrast, not promised to any individual.

## Analysis

### Medical: SORT Analysis
This is one of the more epistemically careful videos in the batch. The vitamin D section is exemplary: he cites the **null VIOLET trial** (high-dose vitamin D during critical illness didn't help) to argue the benefit is in *baseline* status, not crisis rescue — and supports steady levels with a large RCT meta-analysis (~12% lower cancer mortality) while explicitly labeling the pre-op/ICU associations as observational ("a bit of both"). The microbiome→immunotherapy claim references the real 2018 *Science* melanoma work and stem-cell-transplant survival data. The muscle/sarcopenia outcome data (ICU mortality ~2×; a 39-study cancer meta-analysis with higher toxicity and mortality in low-muscle patients) is accurate and clinically meaningful. The insulin-sensitivity-drops-during-infection point is correct. The framing is patient-oriented (survival, recovery, function). The only soft spots are the inherent confounding in observational nutrition/muscle data, which he names himself. No selling beyond the general longevity ethos.

## Summary

The video contrasts five cream cheese brands that use hydrocolloid stabilizer systems (carrageenan, xanthan gum, guar gum) to simulate cream cheese texture at lower cost, versus five brands that use traditional fermentation with minimal ingredients (milk, cream, salt, cultures). Philadelphia Spread, Great Value, 365 by Whole Foods, Dairy Farmers of America private labels, and Arla Foods are flagged. Organic Valley, NYO's, Cultura Supernatural, Green Valley Creamery, and Vermont Creamery are recommended.

#### Total Evidence
The core claims are about food labeling and ingredient composition, not health outcomes. These claims are verifiable against publicly available ingredient lists and FDA regulations. The FDA does maintain separate standards for "cream cheese" vs. "cream cheese spread" (21 CFR 133.133 and 133.134). Hydrocolloids (xanthan gum, carrageenan, guar gum) are FDA-approved food additives with GRAS status. The video accurately represents their function (water binding, viscosity creation) without making unsupported health danger claims about them.

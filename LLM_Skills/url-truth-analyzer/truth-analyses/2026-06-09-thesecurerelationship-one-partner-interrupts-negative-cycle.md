## Analysis

### Science: Claim Validation
The "negative cycle" is the validated core construct of EFT, whose Stage 1 (de-escalation) is precisely about partners recognizing and interrupting that self-reinforcing pursue-withdraw pattern [1]. The claim that one partner's shift (less reactivity, more curiosity) can change the emotional environment is consistent with dyadic/co-regulation findings — partners' states are bidirectionally linked, so one person's regulation can down-regulate the system [2]. The honest framing ("more often one partner starts") avoids overpromising. Caveat the post itself acknowledges in spirit: durable change typically requires both partners eventually engaging; a single regulated partner can stabilize but not unilaterally guarantee transformation.

## Verdict
C1 is the strongest claim — interrupting the negative cycle is the empirically validated entry point of the most evidence-based couples therapy [1]. C2 is well-grounded but graded Moderate because "one partner shifts → the other shifts" is a probabilistic tendency, not a guarantee. Recommend "Yes": accurate, hopeful without overpromising, and actionable. Citation [1] is title-match verified; [2] verified directly.

## ELI5 — Friend to Friend
You don't need your partner to magically change at the same time as you — usually one person gets a little calmer and more curious first, and that quietly changes the whole vibe. It's true and it's encouraging. Pick one reaction you tend to fire off in a fight and try pausing on it this week. Just don't expect to carry the whole relationship alone forever.

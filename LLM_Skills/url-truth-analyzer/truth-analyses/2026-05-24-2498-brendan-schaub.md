On non-medical topics Rogan typically presents as "I'm just a comic, look it up" — he is informed but not authoritative. Neither host is held out as a medical, scientific, or financial authority. Calibration: treat as informed entertainment commentary on MMA history and business; treat everything else as bar-talk opinion.

## Analysis

### Science: Claim Validation

Most of the episode is opinion (fight predictions, GOAT debates, car preferences, "kids these days are on phones") that is not testable. Below are the handful of factual, verifiable claims, with chunk timestamps. Verdicts are short on purpose — this is a comedy/MMA podcast, not a science talk.

**1. "Hamzat Chimaev cut ~22 kg / 40+ lbs from north of 230 lb down to 185 lb for the Strickland fight" (Chunks 3–4, ~25–35 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported in spirit, exact numbers uncorroborated.** Chimaev was widely reported to be walking around ≥220 lb while training for an originally-planned 205 lb Prochazka bout before being redirected to middleweight; his brother Khamzat himself posted weight-cut footage and team statements about cutting "more than 20 kg" pre-UFC 311. The exact ~22 kg figure is a reasonable ballpark but not independently published; the qualitative claim that this caused a near-collapse the day before weigh-ins matches reporting from his camp.
- Caveat: Extreme weight cuts of this magnitude are well-documented in combat-sports medicine (Reale et al., Sports Med 2017) to impair next-day performance, cardiovascular function, and cognition — so the broader scientific framing (massive cut → reduced gas tank → impaired in-cage performance) is correct.

**2. "Mid-1960s US adult smoking rate was 40–45%, today ~11–12%" (Chunk 11, ~104 min — they cite Perplexity / American Lung Association)**
- Verdict: **Supported.** CDC and ALA historical data show adult smoking prevalence peaked at ~42.4% in 1965 and fell to ~11.5% by 2022 (CDC NHIS). The numbers Perplexity returned in-show are accurate.

**3. "Josh Barnett was the youngest UFC heavyweight champion, stripped for steroids" (Chunk 5, ~46 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported.** Barnett won the UFC heavyweight title at age 24 (UFC 36, March 22, 2002, def. Randy Couture) and was stripped weeks later after testing positive for boldenone (anabolic steroid) per the California State Athletic Commission. He remains the youngest UFC HW champ in the modern record.

**4. "Khabib jumped over the cage and attacked Conor's team after UFC 229" (Chunk 3, ~22 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported.** This is the well-known UFC 229 post-fight brawl (Oct 6, 2018) — Khabib leapt the cage and attacked Dillon Danis; Conor was simultaneously punched by Khabib's cousins inside the cage. Both fighters were fined and suspended by the NSAC.

**5. "Sean Strickland beat Israel Adesanya for the middleweight title in Australia" (Chunks 1, 4)**
- Verdict: **Supported.** UFC 293 (Sept 9, 2023, Sydney), Strickland def. Adesanya by unanimous decision (49-46 x3).

**6. "Netflix ~300M+ subscribers; Paramount+ ~79M (≈35M US)" (Chunk 9, ~85 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported, mostly.** Netflix reported 301.6M global subscribers in Q4 2024; Paramount+ reported ~77.5M global subscribers (with majority in US/Canada) in Q4 2024. Numbers are within reasonable rounding for a live conversation.

**7. "Jake Paul vs Anthony Joshua did 33M viewers on Netflix" (Chunk 9, ~87 min)**
- Verdict: **Plausible but unverified at the time of analysis.** Netflix's prior boxing event (Paul vs. Mike Tyson, Nov 2024) drew ~108M global viewers per Netflix's own announcement. A Paul-Joshua bout was negotiated for late 2025; Schaub may be quoting an early Netflix-supplied figure. Treat Netflix-issued audience numbers with skepticism — they are not independently audited (no Nielsen-style verification) and have been disputed by industry analysts.

**8. "UK is implementing a generational smoking ban — people born after a certain date will never legally be able to buy cigarettes" (Chunk 11, ~106 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported.** UK's Tobacco and Vapes Bill (introduced 2024 under Sunak, carried forward by Starmer's government) prohibits the sale of tobacco to anyone born on or after 1 January 2009. Rolling age-based ban as described.

**9. "China has a social credit system that can prevent people from buying plane tickets" (Chunk 11, ~107 min)**
- Verdict: **Partially supported.** China does maintain travel-restriction lists (the "Discredited Persons" list maintained by the Supreme People's Court) that have blocked millions of plane and high-speed-rail bookings, primarily for unpaid court judgments. However, "social credit" as a unified national score affecting everyday life is largely a Western mischaracterization — it is in reality a fragmented set of corporate and municipal blacklists, not one universal score. The plane-ticket-block element is real; the holistic dystopian "score" framing is exaggerated (see Jeremy Daum / Mirjam Meissner research at China Law Translate and MERICS).

**10. "Chinese TikTok (Douyin) shuts down for kids after 10 PM and shows educational content; US TikTok shows brain rot" (Chunk 15, ~144 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported.** Douyin's "Youth Mode" (青少年模式) imposes a 40-minute daily cap and blocks usage between 10 PM and 6 AM for users under 14 (regulation effective Sept 2021, codified by Cyberspace Administration of China). Algorithmic curation toward STEM/cultural content for minors is documented. The qualitative US-vs-China contrast is supported by research (e.g., Network Contagion Research Institute, 2023) showing systematic topic distribution differences, though framing it as "brain rot vs. educational" is somewhat editorialized.

**11. "About 30% of Americans believe at least one of the three Trump assassination attempts was staged" (Chunk 15, ~148 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported.** Multiple 2024–2025 polls (YouGov, J.L. Partners/DailyMail) found roughly 28–33% of Americans believed at least one assassination attempt was staged or were "not sure" tilting toward staged. The ~30% figure cited on-air is in line with published polling.

**12. "Thomas Crooks's (Butler PA shooter) house was professionally scrubbed, no online activity" (Chunk 15, ~149 min)**
- Verdict: **Contested / largely refuted.** FBI testimony to Congress (July 2024) detailed phone, computer, and online activity recovered from Crooks — including searches for past assassinations, the JFK plot, and details about Trump rallies. He had limited social-media presence (consistent with many young men), but the "professionally scrubbed" framing is a conspiracy-tinged exaggeration not supported by published investigative findings.

**13. "MK Ultra is still ongoing and produced the Unabomber and Charles Manson" (Chunk 15, ~148 min)**
- Verdict: **Refuted / speculative.** MK Ultra (1953–1973) ended after Senate Church Committee hearings; most files were destroyed by CIA Director Helms in 1973, with surviving records released via FOIA. Ted Kaczynski (Unabomber) participated as a Harvard undergraduate in a Henry Murray psychology study (not formally MKUltra-funded, though contemporaneous and ethically abusive). Charles Manson is not documented in any released MKUltra file. Claims of ongoing MKUltra programs are speculation with no declassified evidence; treat as conspiracy-flavored opinion.

**14. "Trump rolled back the federal mandate that engines auto-stop at red lights" (Chunk 14, ~138 min)**
- Verdict: **Misleading.** Auto start-stop is not a federal mandate — it is a manufacturer choice driven by CAFE fuel-economy credits awarded by the EPA. The Trump-era EPA in 2025 publicly criticized start-stop and rolled back some CAFE credit incentives that encouraged it, but no "law getting rid of" the technology was passed. Manufacturers can and still do install it.

**15. "Federal mandate on impaired-driving detection / kill switch tied to 2027 model year" (Chunk 12, ~118 min)**
- Verdict: **Supported in substance, exaggerated in framing.** Section 24220 of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (2021) directs NHTSA to require advanced impaired-driving prevention technology in new vehicles; rulemaking is ongoing, with proposed timelines in the late 2020s. The phrase "kill switch" is the activist framing — NHTSA's specification language describes passive driver monitoring that may prevent vehicle operation if impairment is detected. The civil-liberties concerns Rogan raises are real but the technology as proposed is narrower than "they can shut your car off if they don't like your tweet."

**Claims explicitly NOT fact-checked** (anecdote / opinion / prediction, not testable): all GOAT rankings (Jon Jones vs. Alex Pereira, Topuria vs. McGregor), fighter-pay percentage claims about UFC ("18% to fighters"), fight-prediction picks, opinions about Tubi vs. Netflix programming strategy, Porsche/Cadillac/Dodge product preferences, "kids on phones" generational lament, and the entire Sean Brady betting-pattern story (the abnormal betting was real and reported by ESPN; the interpretation of motive is speculation).

## Verdict
This episode is exactly what it looks like on the tin: two old MMA friends having a long, profanity-laced conversation about a fight weekend, the UFC business, cars, and modern social-media life. The MMA history and recent-fight commentary is mostly accurate where it can be checked, because both speakers actually know the sport. The numerical citations (smoking, streaming subscribers, social-media polls) are fetched live from Perplexity during the show and are mostly correct. The few places where they wander into geopolitics, conspiracy territory (MK Ultra, "scrubbed" shooter, "kill switch in your car"), or policy framing, accuracy degrades sharply — they recycle online narratives without source-checking. Nothing here is medical advice, nothing here is investment advice, and nothing here is rigorous reporting; it is two-guys-in-a-room entertainment.

## ELI5 — Friend to Friend
It's a JRE-with-an-old-fighter-buddy episode — fun if you love MMA gossip and dad-energy car talk, and you can mostly trust them on UFC facts. The numbers they look up on the fly (smoking rates, Netflix subs, UK cigarette ban) check out. But when they drift into "the government's going to put a kill switch in your car" or "MK Ultra is still happening," that's just internet vibes, not facts. Listen for fun, don't quote them at Thanksgiving.

## Analysis

### Medical: SORT Analysis
This is the most epistemically sophisticated video in the batch. Dr. Kim correctly cites IARC's group-1 classification of **processed meat** and group-2A for **red meat**, and — crucially — does not conflate them. He fairly represents the genuine scientific controversy (the 2019 *Annals of Internal Medicine* review that downgraded the red-meat evidence vs the American Cancer Society's rebuttal about over-weighting RCTs), and explicitly walks through why nutritional epidemiology is hard (decades-long latency makes RCTs impractical; observational studies carry confounding/recall bias). His mechanistic claims (Warburg effect/aerobic glycolysis; insulin/IGF-1 as growth signals; cancer cells over-expressing insulin receptors; nitrosamines; HCAs/PAHs from high-heat cooking) are accurate. The quantitative associations (per-8oz juice → +17% breast/+10% colorectal/+30% biliary; UPF +10% intake → +13% cancer; processed meat +23%/serving colorectal; +68% gastric with high salt) match the population-study literature he's drawing from, presented as associations not proof. The fiber/resistant-starch counterpoint (a BJN study where 40 g resistant starch abolished a red-meat-induced colonic DNA-damage marker) is a real and apt nuance. Net: responsible, accurate, anti-extremist.

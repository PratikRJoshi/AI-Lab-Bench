## Analysis

### Medical: SORT Analysis
The biological framing is mainstream and accurate: ~1%/yr testosterone decline from ~age 30; perimenopausal estrogen drop; mitochondrial decline/oxidative stress; "inflammaging" (IL-6, TNF-α); visceral fat as an endocrine organ; muscle GLUT4-mediated, partly insulin-independent glucose disposal. The therapeutic levers are POEM-oriented (function, independence) and well-supported (resistance training works even in the very old; aerobic exercise drives mitochondrial biogenesis). The most consequential and well-handled claim is the **HRT/WHI reframing** (C5): Dr. Kim correctly notes the Women's Health Initiative's absolute risks were small and miscommunicated, and that the "timing hypothesis" (starting MHT within ~10 years of menopause) shows a more favorable — even protective — profile, while explicitly avoiding a blanket "everyone should take HRT" claim and deferring to labs + physician. This matches current menopause-society consensus. Appropriately hedged throughout; defers to clinical testing for TRT/MHT.

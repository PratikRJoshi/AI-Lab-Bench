## Analysis
### Medical: SORT Analysis

**Claim under analysis** (implicit, derived from the text overlay + channel framing): *"Hashimoto's thyroiditis can be reversed — and this channel will show you how."*

Pass 1 — four lenses:

- **Safety.** The headline harm pathway is patients with confirmed overt hypothyroidism discontinuing levothyroxine on the strength of a social-media testimonial. Untreated overt hypothyroidism is associated with dyslipidemia, myxedema risk, infertility, and — directly relevant given this channel's fertility origin story — miscarriage, preterm delivery, and adverse neurocognitive outcomes in offspring during pregnancy. The reel does not warn against deprescribing; the channel name itself ("healed") implies it is achievable.
- **Outcomes (POEM vs DOE).** The literature that does support nutritional intervention in Hashimoto's measures **disease-oriented endpoints** — anti-TPO antibody titers, anti-Tg antibodies, TSH, fT4. These are DOEs. **Patient-oriented outcomes** ("durable drug-free euthyroidism," "cure," "remission") are not population-level supported. The teaser implies a POEM ("reverse") on the strength of DOE evidence.
- **Risk of bias.** Single self-reported success story → personal brand → paid program. Selection bias, survivorship bias, and a financial conflict of interest all push in the same direction. No registry data, no cohort, no RCT replication of the personal narrative.
- **Total evidence.** Selenium ~200 µg/day as an adjunct to levothyroxine has Grade B support for reducing anti-TPO titers (a DOE). Vitamin D repletion where deficient is reasonable. Gluten elimination has signal in concurrent celiac/NCGS patients only. "Mycotoxin detox" as a Hashimoto's intervention has no Grade A/B support and is not in any major endocrine guideline. The strong form ("reverse Hashimoto's without levothyroxine") has no Grade A/B support.

Pass 2 — SORT grade:

- **Grade B** for the narrow DOE claim "selenium + anti-inflammatory diet can lower anti-TPO antibody titers as an adjunct."
- **Grade C** for the implied POEM claim "you can reverse your Hashimoto's" (the meaning the reel's overlay actually conveys to a lay viewer). This is the operative grade, because that is the claim the post makes.

Justification: The strong, patient-facing form of the claim relies on disease-oriented surrogate evidence to back a patient-oriented outcome the literature does not establish, against guideline-recommended pharmacotherapy with a strong safety record.

## Verdict
